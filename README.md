# Claude AI Workflows & Mindmaps
Open-source collection of Claude workflows, prompts, and visual mindmaps by @rajkishor

### 🚀 What is this?
I share free, visual AI education to help solo founders and students build with Claude without a big team.

### 📚 What's inside?
- **/workflows** - Claude agent workflows (research agents, automation)
- **/prompts** - Tested prompts for deep research & building
- **/mindmaps** - Handwritten-style mindmaps for books & AI concepts (Rich Dad Poor Dad, etc.)
- **/templates** - Notion + LinkedIn content templates made with Claude

### 🎯 Impact
- 50K+ learners across LinkedIn / Instagram
- 10K+ saves monthly on free resources
- Focus on Tier 2/3 city students in India

### 🛠️ How to use
1. Copy any prompt from /prompts
2. Paste in Claude
3. Build your own version
